amazon.add_gift_wrapping_to_product  [POST /amazon/cart/{product_id}/gift_wrap]
Add gift wrapping to a product in your cart. If the product is already set to be gift wrapped, its quantity will be updated.
  - product_id (integer, required): ID of the product in your cart to be gift wrapped.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from amazon app login.
  - quantity (integer, optional): Quantity of the product to be gift wrapped.
  response: {"message": "string"}
