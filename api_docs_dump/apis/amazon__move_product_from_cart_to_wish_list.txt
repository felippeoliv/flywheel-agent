amazon.move_product_from_cart_to_wish_list  [POST /amazon/cart_to_wish_list/{product_id}]
Move product from the cart to the wish list.
  - product_id (integer, required): ID of the product to be move
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from amazon app login.
  - quantity (integer, optional): Quantity of the product to move.
  response: {"message": "string"}
