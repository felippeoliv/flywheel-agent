amazon.move_product_from_wish_list_to_cart  [POST /amazon/wish_list_to_cart/{product_id}]
Move product from the wish list to the cart.
  - product_id (integer, required): ID of the product to move to cart.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from amazon app login.
  - quantity (integer, optional): Quantity of product to move to cart.
  response: {"message": "string"}
