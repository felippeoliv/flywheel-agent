amazon.update_product_review  [PATCH /amazon/reviews/{review_id}]
Update a product review.
  - review_id (integer, required): ID of the product review.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from amazon app login.
  - rating (integer, optional): Product rating for this review.
  - title (string, optional): Title of the review.
  - text (string, optional): Text content of the review.
  response: {"message": "string"}
