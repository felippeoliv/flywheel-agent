amazon.write_product_review  [POST /amazon/products/{product_id}/reviews]
Write a product review.
  - product_id (integer, required): ID of the product to review.
  - rating (integer, required): Product rating for this review.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from amazon app login.
  - title (string, optional): Title of the review.
  - text (string, optional): Text content of the review.
  response: {"message": "string", "product_review_id": 1}
