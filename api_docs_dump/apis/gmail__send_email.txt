gmail.send_email  [POST /gmail/emails]
Send a new email to one or more recipients.
  - email_addresses (list[string], required): List of recipient email addresses.
  - subject (string, required): The subject of the email.
  - body (string, required): The body of the email.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from gmail app login.
  - attachment_file_paths (list[string], optional): List of absolute file paths (starting with /) from the file_system app to attach to the email.
  - file_system_access_token (string, optional): Access token obtained from file_system app login. Only needed for attachments.
  response: {"message": "string", "sent_email_thread_id": 1, "sent_email_id": 1}
