splitwise.show_activity  [GET /splitwise/account/activity]
Show a history of your expenses and payments combined.
  - access_token (string, required): Access token obtained from splitwise app login.
  - show_expenses (boolean, optional): Whether to show expenses in the history.
  - show_payments (boolean, optional): Whether to show payments in the history.
  - page_index (integer, optional): The index of the page to return.
  - page_limit (integer, optional): The maximum number of results to return per page.
  - sort_by (string, optional): The attribute to sort the expenses/payments by prefixed with +/- to reflect ascending/descending. Valid attributes: created_at.
